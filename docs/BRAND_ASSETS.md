# Brand and Product Assets

## Working identity

- Name: **Daymark** (provisional; trademark/package/domain clearance required).
- Category: Universal Productivity Toolkit.
- Tagline: **Your work, organized. Your AI, connected.**
- Short description: A local-first productivity layer that keeps tasks, focus, and work context consistent across your AI agents.

## Visual direction

Align with the `codewithalbet` technology identity without making Daymark a sub-brand that cannot stand independently.

- Primary navy: `#0B1220`.
- Deep blue: `#102A43`.
- Cyan accent: `#22D3EE`.
- Bright blue: `#38BDF8`.
- Surface: `#F8FAFC`.
- Slate text: `#64748B`.
- Dark text: `#0F172A`.

Use generous whitespace, restrained cyan highlights, clear type hierarchy, and simple line icons. Avoid excessive neon, circuit-board clichés, robot mascots, and fake productivity score gauges.

## Logo concept

An abstract combination of:

- an open notebook/book shape (daily record);
- a small checkmark (completed work);
- a subtle connected node/terminal glyph (AI-agent interoperability).

The mark must remain legible at 16–24 px. Wordmark uses a modern sans-serif. Provide horizontal, stacked, monochrome, and icon-only variants.
